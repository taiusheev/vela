# Family, caregiving, relationships, and family memory apps

## What do 33 verified products suggest for Vela's first week?

### Takeaway
The strongest direction is a personal family space with immediate conversation, a separately scheduled daily ritual, and very small concrete requests for help. Give the organiser something useful to configure and write before anyone accepts an invitation; make the parent's arrival feel like receiving something from their child.

### Cited Findings

Research date: 2026-10-15. Exactly 33 distinct products below. Evidence is public official product/support material, with developer-owned App Store descriptions where official sites did not render. This is a capability and pattern review, not 33 hands-on app audits. “Verified pattern” means the source explicitly describes it; “Vela implication” is our inference. None of these sources establishes that copying a pattern improves retention.

### Inferences

- **First session:** create Mom's family space, set her time/language, write one message in your own words, then invite. Show pending status with a preview of what she will receive. Snug's explicit remote preparation and Storyworth's organiser-edited queue support the pattern; neither proves Vela's implementation. [Snug](https://www.snugsafe.com/), [Storyworth](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-storyworths-questions).
- **Two parallel rhythms:** a persistent “Message Mom” composer for now, and a separate “Tomorrow morning · 8:00 AM Mom's time” card. Qeepsake explicitly combines prompts and spontaneous journaling; Caring Village markets real-time chat beside scheduled care. [Qeepsake](https://qeepsake.com/), [Caring Village](https://caringvillage.com/).
- **Nearby helper invitation:** proposed copy: “Hi [name], I’m [child], [parent]’s [son/daughter]. Would you be a nearby contact if I can’t reach her? You can review what that means here: [link]”. Keep the URL on its own line in actual share text; put details on the destination. Test clickability in Telegram/SMS with a recipient who is not in Contacts. Manual invitation and pending management have a direct Medisafe precedent; a clickable URL in Vela's target messenger still requires real-device proof. [Medisafe](https://help-center.medisafe.com/en/articles/8395236-all-about-medfriends).
- **Human voice:** proposed style settings should come from the organiser's approved messages: greeting, usual name for parent, message length, language, punctuation, emoji preference, preferred topics. Generated drafts should preserve those cues without inventing family facts or pretending a child approved a text they did not. Storyworth verifies editable custom prompts, not automatic style imitation. [Storyworth](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-storyworths-questions).
- **Lit windows:** use seven quiet windows labelled by local day; light one for a defined real interaction and allow tapping to open it. Display “4 days connected this week” rather than threatening a broken streak. Show sent, replied, and read-back states separately. FamilyAlbum's visit/reaction distinction and Hearth's family rhythm summary inspire this; the particular window motif is Vela's own design proposal. [FamilyAlbum](https://family-album.com/), [Hearth](https://hearthdisplay.com/).
- **Header:** name/portrait plus relationship context on the left, one relevant action on the right; reserve a larger brand mark for onboarding or a deliberate home moment. On a pending family, right action might be “Invite”; active conversation could use “Details” or “Call”. Cozi supplies person context and Between combines everyday conversation with shared history; neither source establishes a universal header prescription. [Cozi](https://www.cozi.com/), [Between](https://between.us/).

### Gaps

- No claimed hands-on onboarding, accessibility, native layout, retention, or provider delivery evidence for these 33 products. Public pages cannot establish right-top spacing or icon quality inside their current apps.
- No verified competitor evidence proves that automatic prose-style adaptation improves parent trust. Validate Vela drafts against the founder's real messages and parent feedback.
- No general URL-format guarantee can fix all messenger clients. Test the actual exported helper invitation across new/non-contact Telegram, SMS, copying, and native sharing.
- Morning settings before invite acceptance are directly supported as a design precedent by Snug's setup model, but Vela still needs access-control and activation semantics reviewed in its own source.
- No safety system examined here establishes that failure to reply is an emergency. Any helper escalation needs explicit wording and actual acceptance/delivery states.
- OurHome, the originally intended Carely family product, and HereAfter AI were replaced with Shared, Qeepsake, and FamilySearch Memories because their intended current capabilities could not be confidently verified from public primary sources. Maple remains in the count specifically as a verified sunset/continuity case, not an actively recommended app.
