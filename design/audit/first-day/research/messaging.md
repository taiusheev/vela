# Messaging and community apps: 34-app evidence review

## 1. What can 34 distinct apps teach Vela's first week?

### Takeaway

Make contact with a person immediately available, keep the morning ritual separately editable, and treat invitations as a short consent request with a reliable destination. The table contains **34 distinct apps**, researched through public official pages on 15 October 2026. This is a documentation and public-product-material review, not an installed-app or hands-on UI audit. Each implication is our design inference, not a measured claim about retention or usability.

## 2. What should Vela change in invitations, conversation, and personal voice?

### Takeaway

Design the first day around a first real exchange, with the next morning already prepared. These are proposed Vela decisions inferred from the evidence, not features proven by the research.

### Cited findings

- Real-time and later delivery can share a conversation while keeping draft/queued states explicit. [Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/201457107-Send-and-read-messages), [Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/schedule-text-messages-to-send-later-iph5ae9a7be6/ios).
- A link/QR and a distinct acceptance step are recognizable connection patterns. [Signal](https://signal.org/blog/phone-number-privacy-usernames/), [GroupMe](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/groupme/how-do-i-create-a-share-link-in-groupme), [Reddit](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/39411701270036-How-to-use-Reddit-Chat).
- The sender can be able to compose while the receiver's ability to see a message still depends on contact/request settings. [Snapchat](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/7012328141076-How-to-Start-a-Chat-on-Snapchat), [X](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/direct-messages).
- Telegram documents bot deep links and a start action; bot interaction is a distinct step from merely possessing a URL. [Telegram bot features](https://core.telegram.org/bots/features#deep-linking).

### Inferences

1. **Short helper invitation:** “Hi [name], it's [child]. Mom lives near you. If I can't reach her, could I ask you to check in? See what's involved: [URL]”. This is sample copy, not a source quotation. Keep detailed responsibilities and acceptance on the landing page. Name the sender before Vela. Do not describe this as guaranteed emergency coverage.
2. **Clickable links with fallbacks:** Render actual tappable links in Vela's own screen, share a proper HTTPS URL on its own line, and offer Open invitation, Share invitation, Copy link, and optional QR. Test the receiving provider. Provider anti-spam/request restrictions can outlive correct Vela link generation; explain a fallback rather than promising universal clickability.
3. **Before acceptance:** The parent card says “Waiting for Mom to connect.” Morning time, language, and the first drafted question remain editable. Delivery status says “Starts after Mom connects,” with a separate share/resend action.
4. **Talk now versus Morning:** A persistent composer sends immediately. A distinct morning card edits tomorrow's scheduled note. A queued message displays exact local time and allows edit/cancel/send-now. Do not make the composer a synonym for next-morning scheduling.
5. **One reply affordance:** Use one single-turn reply icon paired with “Reply.” Do not put an arrow in both the icon and label. Preserve the original message context above the composer.
6. **Human voice:** Use the child's own recent approved questions as bounded style examples: forms of address, typical length, vocabulary, punctuation, and language. Prefer minimal editing. Offer an editable draft and an explicit “Sounds like me” feedback action. Do not invent personal memories, feelings, health claims, or events. No reviewed source establishes that silently cloning style improves family trust; this is a hypothesis requiring examples and evaluation.
7. **Purposeful header:** Left/center gives Mom's name and optional portrait; right gives one contextually useful action such as family settings or details. Connection/channel status belongs beneath the name when relevant. The page should earn its identity through typography, warm surfaces, restrained amber light, and the family relationship; a repeated large logo is unnecessary.
8. **First-week sequence:** Connect Mom → write one authentic first note → make the first reply easy → prepare tomorrow → show a warm record of exchanged moments. Keep helper setup available but secondary unless the family needs it immediately.
9. **Lit windows:** Treat a window as an actual exchanged day, not an app-open day or a scheduled draft. Show seven windows with textual dates/states. Avoid turning a missed reply into an alarming red failure or resetting the family history. This is Vela's own proposed identity expression, not copied from a messaging app.

### Gaps

Whether parent-facing copy should explicitly say “From [child], delivered by Vela” needs product and family testing. Natural wording should not falsely imply a child personally typed every automated sentence at send time. The research does not establish a successful automatic style-learning implementation.

## 3. What must still be validated on Vela's real pilot?

### Takeaway

Public documentation can support a design direction, but the user's reported Telegram friction needs reproduction on the actual recipient path.

### Cited findings

- Telegram's Spam FAQ describes restrictions related to unsolicited messages, including links and invitations; its general FAQ documents user privacy conditions. [Telegram spam guidance](https://telegram.org/faq_spam), [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq).
- Messenger and Snapchat document separate pre-acceptance behaviors. These show why provider-specific testing matters, but are not evidence of the cause of the user's Telegram issue. [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/01/introducing-stricter-message-settings-for-teens-on-instagram-and-facebook/), [Snapchat](https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/7012328141076-How-to-Start-a-Chat-on-Snapchat).

### Inferences

Validate: organiser iPhone → helper who is not a contact; new parent before bot Start; parent after acceptance; copied link; native share; opening from SMS and Telegram; expired/revoked link; edit morning before acceptance; send now while a morning message is queued; a failed send and retry; and one real family reply. Preserve actual delivery, acknowledgement, and read-back as different states. Device/provider screenshots and observations should be private and redact invite tokens.

### Gaps

- The exact cause of nonclickable helper links is unknown. Possible rendering, URL formatting, and provider contact restrictions must be distinguished experimentally.
- No current Vela screen, source code, delivery logs, or live family account was inspected by this research subtask.
- No claim of a 100-app hands-on audit is justified. This file contributes exactly 34 public-source app reviews to the coordinator's broader 100-app research set.
