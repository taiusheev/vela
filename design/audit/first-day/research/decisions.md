# Make Vela Feel Like Family Again

Vela should open a family conversation immediately, keep the morning ritual separately editable, and make the first week a visible record of real messages. The founder's eleven observations describe a connected problem: setup and scheduled automation currently stand between people and contact with their family. The proposed experience makes Mom—not Vela's logo or a generated questionnaire—the center of each screen. A short invitation, useful preparation before acceptance, an always-available composer, and warm daily windows give the first session a concrete purpose. Personalization should preserve the child's actual words and offer better drafts without falsely attributing unattended messages to them. These are **proposed design decisions informed by exactly 100 distinct products**, not completed engineering or demonstrated retention improvements.

## 100 products point toward immediate, personal contact

Research checked on **15 October 2026** covers 34 messaging/community apps, 33 family/care/relationship products, and 33 ritual/journal/habit apps. The three numbered matrices contain 100 rows and 100 distinct product names; aliases count once. Kinzoo Messenger and Kinzoo Together are separately named apps; Apple Messages and Apple Fitness are distinct products. Maple is explicitly a product-lifecycle case because its official site announces a 31 December 2026 sunset, rather than an active-service recommendation. Each row links official product, help, developer, or publisher material and states its evidence limit. **This is public capability research, not 100 hands-on audits.** Current screen geometry, accessibility, delivery behavior, and first-week outcomes were not measured.

The strongest pattern is separating **what someone wants to say from when it should arrive**. Slack documents immediate sending, drafts, and scheduled messages; Apple Messages lets users edit a queued message or send it immediately. Qeepsake combines daily prompts with contributions whenever desired. These capabilities support a Vela conversation available all day beside a morning note; they do not establish that any copied layout improves retention. ([Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/201457107-Send-and-read-messages), [Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/schedule-text-messages-to-send-later-iph5ae9a7be6/ios), [Qeepsake](https://qeepsake.com/))

Preparation before the recipient arrives is a useful second pattern. Snug explicitly allows preparing a loved one's account before they download and verify their phone. Medisafe documents manual invitations, pending invitations, resend, and cancellation. Vela should therefore let the organiser finish useful work while Mom is pending, with activation clearly distinguished from saving preferences. This is a design inference; it does not verify Vela's authorization or scheduler behavior. ([Snug](https://www.snugsafe.com/), [Medisafe](https://help-center.medisafe.com/en/articles/8395236-all-about-medfriends))

## A family header and two clear rhythms replace the dashboard

**Proposed design:** the family home opens with Mom's preferred name, portrait or initials, her local time, and one contextual action at the top right. Pending connection uses “Invite”; an active relationship uses “Details.” The main content shows the latest real exchange, a compact seven-window week, and “Tomorrow morning · 8:00 AM Mom's time.” A persistent “Message Mom” action opens the conversation; the thread keeps an ordinary composer available. Morning configuration lives in its own clearly named sheet with timing, language, next question, and a recipient preview. A first session with no conversation shows the organiser's saved first note and a clear waiting state rather than empty history.

Identity comes through cream surfaces, charcoal text, restrained amber light, readable Literata family moments, and Inter controls. Larger brand marks belong to welcome and deliberate brand moments. Person-based organization and shared history have precedents in Cozi and Between; Locket places close relationships in a recurring personal surface. These sources support the direction, not a claim that they use the exact proposed header or typography. ([Cozi](https://www.cozi.com/), [Between](https://between.us/), [Locket](https://help.locket.com/en/articles/14225418-my-teen-asked-me-to-get-locket-what-is-it))

Proposed helper share text, with the actual URL on a separate line:

> Hi [name], it's [child]. If I can't reach Mom, could I ask you to check in on her? Here's what being a nearby contact involves:
>
> [invitation URL]

The landing page explains who is asking, what access acceptance grants, how to decline or leave, and that joining does not accept a current task. Telegram documents deep links and the bot Start step, but that does not guarantee every noncontact client makes every link clickable. The recipient flow must prove connection separately from merely opening the URL. ([Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq#q-how-does-t-me-work), [Telegram bot deep links](https://core.telegram.org/bots/features#deep-linking))

## The first week should accumulate real moments

**First-day journey:** choose Mom's preferred name and channel, confirm her timezone and morning, write a first note in your own words, preview her experience, and share the invitation. Everything remains editable while waiting. On acceptance, review and send the saved note immediately; leave tomorrow's morning visible and separate. When Mom answers, open the actual text or voice message with one Reply action and an optional heart. Do not require nearby-helper setup before this first exchange. Remento's familiar-channel responses and Storyworth's editable questions support reducing recipient setup and preserving the organiser's wording. ([Remento](https://www.remento.co/), [Storyworth](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-storyworths-questions))

**Days two and three:** make the next morning easy to edit and show the latest reply first. Ask for an optional style example only when useful; do not announce that Vela has learned someone's voice from one sentence. **Days four through six:** offer one editable suggestion once enough authored examples exist; introduce a nearby helper if the organiser wants one. On a quiet day, keep conversation available and use factual status rather than a broken-streak warning or an inference about Mom's wellbeing. **Day seven:** open the week's windows into actual messages and offer a simple morning adjustment or pause. This sequence is a proposal to evaluate with families, not a researched causal formula for retention.

For the first version, **a window means “a day with a family message,” not proof of reciprocal connection or wellbeing**. Light today's window for a recorded parent contribution or a successfully sent organiser-written/explicitly approved message. Keep attribution and reliable event evidence; exclude app opens, future scheduling, and unattended auto-generated questions. A late reply lights its actual parent-local day. Earlier windows remain lit after missed days, and the weekly label says “4 days with messages.” If a future version says “days connected,” define and require a genuinely reciprocal event. Gentler Streak's rest states and Apple Fitness's pause behavior support continuity without erasing history; neither source defines family connection for Vela. ([Gentler Streak](https://gentlerstories.com/gentlerstreak), [Apple Fitness](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/adjust-your-activity-ring-goals-iph9a08e004e/ios))

## Personal voice needs provenance and a staged rollout

**Proposed authorship contract:** distinguish “written by you,” “draft approved by you,” and “automatic morning message.” Keep unattended automation an understandable choice for both organiser and parent, with a short explanation at setup and unambiguous sender context. Natural wording should not depend on attaching a technical disclaimer to every sentence, but the system must never pretend the child personally wrote or approved something they did not. A style profile can use the organiser's chosen name for Mom, language, typical length, greeting, punctuation, and emoji habits. Store exact authored examples, permit removal, and exclude rejected or unedited generated drafts so the system does not learn its own generic output. Reviewed competitors establish editable questions and saved prompts, not reliable imitation of a family member's voice. ([Storyworth](https://welcome.storyworth.com/blog/a-complete-guide-to-storyworths-questions), [Reflect](https://reflect.app/))

Roll out in dependency order: first make invitation actions/copy and pending preference saves reliable; next review an interactive prototype covering pending setup, conversation, morning editing, helper acceptance, and window history; then implement approved navigation and identity changes in coordination with the existing messenger work. Add authorship provenance and suggestion evaluation before enabling adaptive voice, and define trustworthy interaction events before shipping window counts. The founder subsequently explicitly directed this first-day direction to replace PR95. Implementation status and release evidence are recorded in apps/app/docs/first-day-experience.md. This report does not certify any backend fix or UI implementation.

Required evidence remains: appropriate repository checks for the actual changes; simulator checks for navigation, drafts, keyboard, large text, and narrow layouts; signed-iPhone and VoiceOver checks; real Telegram/new-noncontact and SMS sharing, opening, Start/acceptance, revoke/expiry, and retry tests; pending-preference authorization and activation tests; timezone/day-boundary and duplicate-event tests; and a first-week family pilot examining whether people actually understand authorship and return to real replies. Provider delivery, recipient acknowledgement, and organiser read-back must stay distinct. Track invitation acceptance, first genuine exchange, early returns, and draft acceptance without claiming those observations establish retention until a baseline and adequate pilot evidence exist.
