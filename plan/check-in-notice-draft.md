# Check-in: privacy notice wording (DRAFT, needs the founder's yes)

## Lines to change in privacy-notice (en)

- "Voice is never required: every ask can be answered with one tap."
  → "Voice is never required. Her morning is answered in her own words; if it is still unanswered after a few hours, Vela sends a short check-in that she can answer with one tap."

## Lines to add (under what Vela does with answers)

- "**The check-in.** When the person has not answered their morning after about two and a half hours, Vela asks how they are, with two buttons: "I'm okay" and "Not okay, tell my family". If they tap "Not okay", Vela tells every organiser at once by message, and the founder, with the time they tapped it. The family group sees that they are not feeling okay. Vela does not ask why and never guesses. Vela is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call for help directly."
