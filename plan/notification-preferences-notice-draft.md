# Draft privacy-notice.v11 addition — not approved or served

The new individual notification controls remain `NOTIFICATION_CONTROLS=off` in development, staging and production. Tests can explicitly enable them with synthetic accounts. Enabling collection needs founder approval of the final notice, publication through the normal notice generator and version/configuration update, and telling affected families first. Do not change the served v10 notice as part of this proposal.

Proposed English addition under account/app setup:

**Notification choices:** You may choose whether Vela sends a notification when a parent answers your question and whether it reminds you before your turn. Vela saves those choices with your account to honour them across your phones. They are not shown to other family members or sent to an AI provider. You can change them in Settings. Vela clears the saved choices when your account is deleted. They do not switch off Vela Light quiet-morning notices, which are explained separately.

Proposed Traditional Chinese addition (native review pending):

**通知偏好：** 您可以選擇是否接收父母回答問題的通知，以及輪到您準備問題前的提醒。Vela 會將這些選擇儲存在您的帳號中，讓您在不同手機上都使用相同設定。其他家庭成員看不到這些選擇，Vela 也不會將其傳給 AI 服務供應商。您可以隨時在「設定」中更改。刪除帳號時，Vela 會清除這些通知偏好。這些選擇不會關閉另行說明的 Vela Light 早晨未回覆通知。

No new parent consent is requested: these are the signed-in person's own notification settings. No answer content, health information, analytics or new recipient is introduced. Existing account deletion clears them; the migration copies existing routine on/off choices without opting anyone back in. An older app's combined on/off switch continues to apply to both choices.
