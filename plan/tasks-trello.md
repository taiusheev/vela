# Trello board: Vela · Phase 0

Trello has no CSV import on the free plan, but pasting several lines into "Add a card" creates one card per line. Make four lists named as below, click "Add a card" in each, paste the block, and press Enter. Then add a "Done" list on the right.

Board name: **Vela · Phase 0 (prove the loop)**
Labels to create: `founder` (green), `co-founder` (yellow), `blocked` (red)

## List: Week 1 · survey out, first interviews

```
[founder] Create the Telegram bot, Cloudflare account, Anthropic key (20 min, bot/README.md)
[founder] Decide repo visibility: public or private
[founder] Post the survey in three places today
[founder] Book 8 interviews from survey respondents who opted in
[founder] Show your parent a mock of the morning arrival; write down her exact words
[co-founder] Update survey intro and recruitment posts for the all-families framing
[co-founder] Prototype changes for phase 0: family content in the arrival, quiet notice to the child, away mode
[co-founder] Landing page with waitlist and two price points
[co-founder] Weekly review template and decisions log in the repo
```

## List: Week 2 · interviews, close pilot families

```
[founder] Interviews 9–16, each ending with the pilot offer ($15, refundable, starts Monday)
[founder] 10 families confirmed: eldest + organiser + one under-30; messengers, wake times, two nearby contacts
[co-founder] Deploy the prototype; three-day test with the founder's own parent
[co-founder] Onboarding script: five minutes with the child, five with the parent, in Russian
[co-founder] Interview synthesis: objections, reasons to say yes, prices named, wish/worry clustering
```

## List: Week 3 · pilot live

```
[founder] Onboard 10–15 families; do the parent call yourself for the first five
[founder] Daily 15-minute check on the loop; log every false quiet notice and parent complaint
[founder] Write the weekly read by hand for each family on Sunday (AI drafts, you edit)
[founder] Interview anyone who declined or dropped
[co-founder] Daily metrics: answer rate, latency, family-content days, quiet notices and whether true, opt-outs
```

## List: Week 4 · measure and decide

```
[founder] Day-14 check-in with every family: "If Vela stopped tomorrow, how would you feel?"
[founder] Ask each parent through the chat: «Нравится ли вам получать это по утрам?»
[founder] Ask each paying family for two introductions
[co-founder] Phase-0 report: metrics, what the arrival must contain, what the weekly read must say, MVP scope
[co-founder] Pitch deck v1 from phase-0 evidence
[founder] Decision: build the MVP, or change one variable and rerun phase 0
```

Kill signals (put on the board description): fewer than 4 families paying after 20 interviews; more than 30% of parents refusing or going silent in week one for reasons other than being away; family-content days under 30% by week two despite prompts.
