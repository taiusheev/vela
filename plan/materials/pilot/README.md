## Who can join the pilot

Decided on 17 September 2026 (decision L12, `legal-memo.md` Q6, C23), until a lawyer advises otherwise:

- **Recorded for every family, the founder's own included,** before the organiser's setup: for the person the light is for and for each family member who will be in the family group, the country where they live (for the United States, the state) and whether they are a Russian citizen. The founder asks the organiser, and files the answers in the Google Drive notes under the family code, as a country code and yes or no, never with names.
- **Onboarded:** only a family in which everyone lives in Taiwan or in a US state other than Washington, and no one is a Russian citizen.
- **Waiting:** every other family, until the steps in `legal-memo.md` Q6 for that place are done (the EU: explicit consent, a representative and transfer tools; Washington: separate consents to collect and to share health data, and a health data policy; Kazakhstan and Russia: a lawyer's advice). The founder tells the organiser kindly that the family has to wait.
- **The founder's own family** is checked the same way, and waits like any other if it does not qualify; the founder's own use of Vela is then limited to staging with test accounts, which hold no real family data.
- A family member who moves, or a new adult added to the group, is checked the same way before they join the group.

## Onboarding a family, in order

The order matters: Vela links a group only when the bot is added by an organiser whose setup is finished (flows §3.3), and it sends its consent message only when the person opens the invite link that setup produces (flows §3.2).

1. **Organiser says yes to the pilot.** Check who can join (above). Send the organiser agreement and the privacy notice in their language. They reply "I agree". In the "Vela pilot" folder in Google Drive, under the family code, note only the date, the channel, the text versions and that they agreed; their words go into Vela, in the `pilot` and `privacy_notice` rows written in step 3, once their member row exists. Nothing is paid: the pilot is free (decision 13).
2. **Organiser talks to the person first.** The founder's call is never the first they hear of Vela.
3. **Organiser sets Vela up in a private chat with the bot.** They send `/start` and answer the questions (name, greeting, language, country, time zone, wake time); at the nearby-contact question they give up to two names, each with how the contact knows the person if they like, or tap **Skip**. Vela takes names only: it refuses a text holding a phone number (`onboarding.nearby_no_number`), and lists no contact in any notice until their yes, with their number, is recorded (step 7). The last message holds the invite link for the person; it works for 7 days and the organiser keeps it until the call. The founder then checks the new family and records the organiser's consent rows (`record_consent` on the admin page; sections A and B of `infra/runbooks/data-requests.md` until the admin page runs in production).
4. **Organiser creates the family group** after step 3: a new Telegram group with family members only and without the person, children under 13 not added, and the Vela bot added by the organiser. **Before adding each adult, now or later, the organiser sends that adult the privacy notice individually, in their language** (the link above), and checks with the founder that the adult can join (above). Vela's first message in the group links the notice in the family's language, with the button "I've read it": each adult who taps it is recorded once (`privacy_notice`, flows §3.3). Asks and replies never wait for the tap. Every week the founder records a `privacy_notice` row for each adult in the group who has not tapped, with the date the organiser sent them the notice (`record_consent`; data-requests B). Making the bot an administrator with every permission switched off is optional and lets reactions count (Telegram only sends reactions to administrator bots). If the bot was added before step 3 was finished, Vela answers "Only the family organiser can connect Vela to a group": remove the bot and add it again.
5. **Onboarding call** with the person, using the consent script in their language, within 7 days of step 3. Record the minimal call note and the `privacy_notice` row for the person (`record_consent`; data-requests B until the admin page runs in production).
6. **Invite link and consent message.** At the end of the call the organiser sends the person the invite link; the person opens it and taps **Start**. Only then does Vela send `consent.request` in the person's language: it says who runs Vela, links the notice in the person's language, and has the buttons "Yes, that's fine" and "No, thank you". The person taps the button themselves; the organiser may only show where it is. Only the tap on yes switches the light on and writes the `light` row. Ten seconds later Vela sends the health-words question (`consent.health_words`) with its own buttons; the person's first tap on it writes the `health_words` row, yes or no, and Vela never asks it again. A tap on No to the light deletes, at once, what setup stored about the person (profile, channel link, invite, nearby contacts), keeps only the decline row, and tells the organisers. If there is no tap in 24 hours, the organiser mentions it once; Vela never reminds. An invited person who never answers is deleted 30 days after their last invite link expired. To invite the person again after a no, or with a fresh profile, the founder uses `create_invite` on the admin page with the organiser (the new link goes to the organiser's own chat).
7. **Nearby contacts.** For a contact named after setup, the founder first adds the name (`add_contact` without a number). The organiser sends text A of the nearby-contact message to each contact and forwards each reply, with the contact's phone number for a yes. The founder records the answer (`record_contact_consent`, with the number for a yes; data-requests C until the admin page runs in production). Vela lists a contact in quiet notices only after a recorded yes (flows §3.12), and a number is stored only while that yes stands. The founder removes a contact who says no within 7 days, and one with no yes 14 days after being added (`remove_contact`; data-requests D).
8. **Research questions** (UCLA-3 at week 0) only after a separate yes on the call.

While the family takes part, the organiser tells the founder about travel, a death, or someone who stops taking part. When a family member leaves the family group, Vela marks them as left by itself (flows §3.16); when an organiser leaves it, nothing changes and the founder is told (`admin.member_left_group`). Organisers have no button for these in the pilot; the founder makes the change the same day on the admin page (`set_away`, `mark_deceased`, `mark_left`, flows §3.17), or by hand until it runs in production (data-requests E, F, G).

On the calls at about day 14 and day 30 the founder asks each organiser "If Vela stopped tomorrow, how would you feel?", and on the day-30 call also whether they would pay for Vela Light at US$9.99 a month. That answer is how the pilot measures willingness to pay while it is free; the answers (yes, no, maybe) go into Google Drive under the family code, without quotes.

## Versions: when to ask again

- Every document carries a version (`privacy-notice.v1`, `consent-script.v1`, `nearby-consent.v1`, `organiser-agreement.v1`, `data-map.v1`). The version is what goes into `consents.text_version`. The in-chat texts carry their own: `consent.request@2` and `consent.health_words@1` (`@vela/copy`, services' `consent.ts`).
- A wording change that keeps the meaning keeps the version. A change of meaning (new data, new purpose, new recipient, longer retention, a new provider that receives family messages) needs a new version, and everyone affected is told before it applies, and asked again where consent covers it.
- English and Traditional Chinese change together and share version numbers.
- The 14 September 2026 revision changed the meaning of the notice, the nearby-contact message and the agreement (contacts stored before their yes, departures noticed, no family content in the founder's Telegram chat, the weekly read's lines, without its counts or suggestion, in "what does the family see") and kept `v1`, because the instrument had not run for any family. - The second 17 September 2026 revision applies decisions L1 to L13 (health words asked separately, the lawful basis per group, consent and deletion records kept 5 years, a No deleting what setup stored, nearby contacts by name until their yes, the "I've read it" button, who can join, minimal notes). ## Words

From spec §9, §20 and research/11: never "monitor", "track", "check on", "keep an eye" (nor "watch over" or "for your safety"); lead with the family; say who acts (the family asks, the organiser calls, Vela carries); use names, never "the parent" or "the user"; no gendered pronoun for the person the light is for; never promise safety; always say Vela is not an emergency service.

## Decisions the pack follows

Decided on 17 September 2026. Decisions 1 to 12 were drafted where the sources are silent and are confirmed as drafted, by the tech co-founder on the founder's instruction. Decision 13 is the founder's own. Decisions 14 to 18 are decisions L1 to L13 of the same day, taken by the tech co-founder on the founder's instruction to decide on the changes `legal-memo.md` recommends (ADR-27, ADR-28).

1. **Retention** for data with no rule in the schema (text of asks, replies and translations, chips, suggestions, `outbound.payload`, `ai_calls.output`): 30 days. Summaries, answer times, quiet-day records and weekly reads: while the family uses Vela. End of pilot without continuing: the family's information deleted within 30 days; kept longer only what already has a longer period (content-free events and daily counts, the AI call log without names or output, error reports, the admin access log, consent and deletion records, research notes under a family code). See `data-map.md`. ADR-24 builds these rules into the retention job.
2. **Research notes and UCLA-3 answers** are filed under a family code and deleted at most 12 months after the pilot ends.
3. **Nearby contacts** may be named at setup, by name and relation only, and appear in no notice until the founder records their yes, which brings their number (flows §3.1, §3.12, decision L8). In the pilot the founder removes a contact who says no within 7 days, and one with no yes 14 days after being added; in the app, a decline or 14 days without an answer deletes them.
4. **Group messages** not meant for Vela are dropped without being stored or logged. Group privacy mode stays on; an administrator bot still receives every group message, and the notice says so.
5. **Real answers in evals or benchmarks** only with a separate yes per item; golden-set text anonymised before commit; voice clips outside git, deleted within 30 days after the benchmark.
6. **The co-founder (an AI coding agent) never receives production message content**; production content is read only by the founder. Every view and action on the admin page is logged automatically (flows §3.17), and reads in the Neon console by hand. The founder's own Telegram chat with the bot carries family names, the name of an organiser who leaves the family group, and links to the admin page, never family content (ADR-21).
7. **Response times:** stop immediately; other requests within 7 days (legal ceilings 15 and 30 days); a withdrawn nearby contact removed within 7 days.
8. **Breach notice** to affected people within 72 hours; the founder tells organisers the same day about a late, missing or wrong message (`infra/runbooks/incident.md`).
9. **New providers** receiving family messages are announced to organisers 14 days ahead.
10. **Error reports** kept at most 90 days in Sentry's EU region; admin access log and daily metrics 24 months.
11. **Manual changes** (consent rows the founder records, nearby contacts, away dates, a death, someone leaving, a new invite, a family's deletion) are made on the admin page once its actions run in production (flows §3.17, build plan 1.12). Until then, and afterwards for what the actions do not cover (a new link for an invite that is still open, a no given on the call, a change to a health-words answer, copies, corrections, deleting one member's information), the founder makes them in the Neon console following `infra/runbooks/data-requests.md`.
12. **Every pilot family is stored in `apac`**, whatever its country, until the region router exists; onboarding does this by itself (ADR-7, updated 14 September 2026).
13. **The pilot is free for families.** There is no fee and no payment of any kind, and no payment data is collected. Vela has no legal entity yet, and payments wait until one exists (plan decision of 13 September 2026); the founder asked for free wherever possible. Willingness to pay is measured instead: on the day-30 call the founder asks each organiser whether they would pay for Vela Light at US$9.99 a month, and, once a paid plan exists, who joins it.
14. **Health words need their own written consent** (L1 to L3, ADR-27). Right after the yes to the light, Vela asks the person separately whether it may pass on their words about health. Without a yes, Vela stores no health mentions, leaves health out of summaries, and a flag reaches the organisers as a note without words; for everyone, summaries and mentions never keep the name of an illness, a test result or a medicine. Saying stop withdraws the yes, and start does not bring it back.
15. **Consent records and deletion proofs** (L4 to L6, ADR-28): a record of a yes or a no outlives the person's data, stripped to reference numbers, the text version, the times and a fingerprint of the text, and is deleted 5 years after it stops applying; a deletion proof hashes only the kind and the id of what was deleted, and is kept 5 years. Consent from a tap on the person's consent messages stores the chat, the message, the values filled into the text and a fingerprint of the rendered text; a tap on "I've read it" stores the chat, the message and the fingerprint of the group greeting taken when the group was linked, with no values filled in.
16. **A no deletes** (L7, ADR-28): a No to the light deletes what setup stored about the person at once, keeping the decline record; an invited person who never answers is deleted 30 days after the last invite link expired; the organiser invites again through the founder (`create_invite`).
17. **The notice reaches every adult** (L9): the organiser sends it individually before adding an adult to the group, each adult may tap "I've read it", and the founder records the notice for each adult who does not.
18. **Who can join, and minimal notes** (L12, L13): families in Taiwan or US states other than Washington with no Russian citizen, until a lawyer advises otherwise; the Google Drive notes hold only codes, dates, versions, yes or no answers and scores.
