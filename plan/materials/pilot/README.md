## Onboarding a family, in order

The order matters: Vela links a group only when the bot is added by an organiser whose setup is finished (flows §3.3), and it sends its consent message only when the person opens the invite link that setup produces (flows §3.2).

1. **Organiser says yes to the pilot.** Send the organiser agreement and the privacy notice in their language. They reply "I agree". Note the date, channel and their words under the family code, in the "Vela pilot" folder in Google Drive; the `pilot` and `privacy_notice` rows are written in step 3, once their member row exists. Nothing is paid: the pilot is free (decision 13).
2. **Organiser talks to the person first.** The founder's call is never the first they hear of Vela.
3. **Organiser sets Vela up in a private chat with the bot.** They send `/start` and answer the questions (name, greeting, language, country, time zone, wake time); at the nearby-contact question they give up to two names and phone numbers, or tap **Skip**. Vela stores those contacts without consent and puts none of them in a notice until their yes is recorded (step 7). The last message holds the invite link for the person; it works for 7 days and the organiser keeps it until the call. The founder then checks the new family and records the organiser's consent rows (sections A and B of `infra/runbooks/data-requests.md`; `record_consent` on the admin page once it ships).
4. **Organiser creates the family group** after step 3: a new Telegram group with family members only and without the person, children under 13 not added, and the Vela bot added by the organiser. Vela's first message in the group links the privacy notice in the family's language; the organiser shares the notice with anyone added to the group later. Making the bot an administrator with every permission switched off is optional and lets reactions count (Telegram only sends reactions to administrator bots). If the bot was added before step 3 was finished, Vela answers "Only the family organiser can connect Vela to a group": remove the bot and add it again.
5. **Onboarding call** with the person, using the consent script in their language, within 7 days of step 3. Record the call note and the `privacy_notice` row for the person (`record_consent` on the admin page once it ships; data-requests B until then).
6. **Invite link and consent message.** At the end of the call the organiser sends the person the invite link; the person opens it and taps **Start**. Only then does Vela send `consent.request` in the person's language with the buttons "Yes, that's fine" and "No, thank you". Only the tap on yes switches the light on and writes the `light` consent row. If there is no tap in 24 hours, the organiser mentions it once; Vela never reminds.
7. **Nearby contacts.** The organiser sends text A of the nearby-contact message to each contact, whether given at setup or named later, and forwards each reply. The founder records the yes or no, adding a contact named later (`add_contact`, `record_contact_consent`; data-requests C until the admin page ships). Vela lists a contact in quiet notices only after a recorded yes (flows §3.12). The founder removes a contact who says no within 7 days, and one with no yes 14 days after being added (`remove_contact`; data-requests D).
8. **Research questions** (UCLA-3 at week 0) only after a separate yes on the call.

While the family takes part, the organiser tells the founder about travel, a death, or someone who stops taking part. When a family member leaves the family group, Vela marks them as left by itself (flows §3.16); when an organiser leaves it, nothing changes and the founder is told (`admin.member_left_group`). Organisers have no button for these in the pilot; the founder makes the change the same day on the admin page (`set_away`, `mark_deceased`, `mark_left`, flows §3.17), or by hand until it ships (data-requests E, F, G).

On the calls at about day 14 and day 30 the founder asks each organiser "If Vela stopped tomorrow, how would you feel?", and on the day-30 call also whether they would pay for Vela Light at US$9.99 a month. That answer is how the pilot measures willingness to pay while it is free; the notes go into Google Drive under the family code.

## Words

From spec §9, §20 and research/11: never "monitor", "track", "check on", "keep an eye" (nor "watch over" or "for your safety"); lead with the family; say who acts (the family asks, the organiser calls, Vela carries); use names, never "the parent" or "the user"; no gendered pronoun for the person the light is for; never promise safety; always say Vela is not an emergency service.

## Decisions the pack follows

Decided on 17 September 2026. Decisions 1 to 12 were drafted where the sources are silent and are confirmed as drafted, by the tech co-founder on the founder's instruction. Decision 13 is the founder's own.

1. **Retention** for data with no rule in the schema (text of asks, replies and translations, chips, suggestions, `outbound.payload`, `ai_calls.output`): 30 days. Summaries, answer times, quiet-day records and weekly reads: while the family uses Vela. End of pilot without continuing: the family's information deleted within 30 days; kept longer only what already has a longer period (content-free events and daily counts, the AI call log without names or output, error reports, the admin access log, deletion proofs, research notes under a family code). See `data-map.md`. ADR-24 builds these rules into the retention job.
2. **Research notes and UCLA-3 answers** are filed under a family code and deleted at most 12 months after the pilot ends.
3. **Nearby contacts** may be given at setup and are stored without consent, but appear in no notice until the founder records their yes (flows §3.1, §3.12). In the pilot the founder removes a contact who says no within 7 days, and one with no yes 14 days after being added; in the app, a decline or 14 days without an answer deletes them.
4. **Group messages** not meant for Vela are dropped without being stored or logged. Group privacy mode stays on; an administrator bot still receives every group message, and the notice says so.
5. **Real answers in evals or benchmarks** only with a separate yes per item; golden-set text anonymised before commit; voice clips outside git, deleted within 30 days after the benchmark.
6. **The co-founder (an AI coding agent) never receives production message content**; production content is read only by the founder. Every view and action on the admin page is logged automatically (flows §3.17), and reads in the Neon console by hand. The founder's own Telegram chat with the bot carries family names, the name of an organiser who leaves the family group, and links to the admin page, never family content (ADR-21).
7. **Response times:** stop immediately; other requests within 7 days (legal ceilings 15 and 30 days); a withdrawn nearby contact removed within 7 days.
8. **Breach notice** to affected people within 72 hours; the founder tells organisers the same day about a late, missing or wrong message.
9. **New providers** receiving family messages are announced to organisers 14 days ahead.
10. **Error reports** kept at most 90 days in Sentry's EU region; admin access log and daily metrics 24 months.
11. **Manual changes** (consent rows the founder records, nearby contacts, away dates, a death, someone leaving, a family's deletion) are made on the admin page once its actions ship (flows §3.17, build plan 1.12). Until then, and afterwards for what the actions do not cover (a new invite link, copies, corrections, deleting one member's information), the founder makes them in the Neon console following `infra/runbooks/data-requests.md`.
12. **Every pilot family is stored in `apac`**, whatever its country, until the region router exists; onboarding does this by itself (ADR-7, updated 14 September 2026).
13. **The pilot is free for families.** There is no fee and no payment of any kind, and no payment data is collected. Vela has no legal entity yet, and payments wait until one exists (plan decision of 13 September 2026); the founder asked for free wherever possible. Willingness to pay is measured instead: on the day-30 call the founder asks each organiser whether they would pay for Vela Light at US$9.99 a month, and, once a paid plan exists, who joins it.
