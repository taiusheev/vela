# Telling families about the photo box (privacy-notice v15)

The founder sends this to each family's organisers who consented under an earlier notice, before the
release that turns the photo box on (ADR-58, ADR-61; notice: "we will tell everyone in the pilot
before the change applies"). Send it in the family's language, from the founder's own account, at
least two days before the release. Families who start after the release read v15 when they agree and
need no separate message. Nothing changes for a family that adds no photos.

The last part is for the organiser to pass on to the parent in their own words; the parent is not
in the family group.

## English

> Hi [name], a small new thing in Vela, coming on [date].
>
> **The photo box.** In the app, under You → Photo box, you can add photos once: new ones (a
> grandchild's drawing, a day out) or old ones (a wedding, your first home). You can write one line
> about each, like "Lily at the beach", and your own question for [parent] about it.
>
> On a morning nobody in the family wrote a question for, about twice a week, Vela sends [parent]
> one of those photos. If you wrote a question for it, it goes as yours, with your name. Otherwise
> Vela asks a short question of its own. Your questions for a morning always come first.
>
> - Only the photos you pick leave your phone. Vela never looks through your photo library.
> - Stuck for a question? Tap "Suggest questions from the photo" and Vela's AI (OpenAI) looks at that one photo and
>   offers three. Only you see them, and nothing is kept. Without that tap, no AI sees your photos.
> - Photos stay in the box until you take them out; then they are deleted within a day.
> - If you add no photos, nothing changes.
>
> The privacy notice is updated (version 15): [link]. Questions: reply here or write to
> support@vela-light.com.
>
> **For [parent], in your own words:** "Some mornings Vela may send you one of our family photos
> with a little question. Just answer in your own words, like always."

## 繁體中文

> [名字] 你好，Vela 有一個小小的新功能，[日期] 開始。
>
> **相片盒。** 在 App 裡的「你 → 相片盒」，照片只要加入一次：新照片（孫子的畫、出遊的照片），或老照片
> （婚禮、第一個家）。每張照片可以寫一句話，例如「Lily 在海邊」，也可以寫一個你想問[長輩稱呼]的問題。
>
> 在家人沒有寫問題的早上，大約一週兩次，Vela 會挑一張照片傳給[長輩稱呼]。如果你替照片寫了問題，就會用
> 你的名義傳出，署上你的名字；沒有寫的話，Vela 會附上一個簡短的問題。你們替某天早上寫的問題永遠優先。
>
> - 只有你挑選的照片會離開你的手機。Vela 不會瀏覽你的相簿。
> - 想不到要問什麼？按「看照片建議問題」，Vela 的 AI（OpenAI）會看這一張照片，提供三個問題。只有你看得到，
>   而且不會保存。沒有按的話，照片不會交給 AI。
> - 照片會留在相片盒裡，直到你把它拿出來；拿出來後一天內刪除。
> - 如果不加入照片，什麼都不會改變。
>
> 隱私權告知事項已更新（第 15 版）：[連結]。有問題請直接回覆，或寫信到 support@vela-light.com。
>
> **請用你自己的話告訴[長輩稱呼]：**「有些早上，Vela 可能會傳一張我們家的照片給您，附上一個小問題。
> 像平常一樣，用您自己的話回答就好。」
