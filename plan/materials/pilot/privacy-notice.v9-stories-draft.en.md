## Line to add to "Memories your family keeps", after "Suggestions"

**Stories.** When you talk about the same thing on a few mornings, Vela may gather those answers into
a story for your family, such as your garden through the season. A story uses only your own words,
exactly as you said them, with a title taken from them; nothing is added or rewritten. To find which
answers belong together, OpenAI (United States) reads the short parts of your ordinary answers that
Vela already picked as memories. A story disappears with its answers after 30 days unless your family
keeps them, and writing **"memories off"** deletes your stories too.
