## Proposed first question

### Would you like Vela to suggest memories from your answers?

Vela can use AI to suggest little things worth remembering: things you enjoy, stories, jokes,
people, moments and plans. Only new answers you send after agreeing are eligible.

Health and other sensitive words are not used for memory suggestions. If Vela is unsure,
it leaves those words out.

Only you can read these memory suggestions. Keeping words saves them privately; it does not share them.
Suggestions expire with the original words, normally 30 days after your answer.
Only you can choose which exact words to keep for longer. Sharing is a separate choice showing who
will be able to read them. New family members do not automatically see older memories.

This is optional. Saying no does not stop your ordinary chats. Agreeing does not start a trial
or charge anyone. You can later withdraw this permission and clear your memories.

Read how this works and the full privacy notice before choosing.

Actions: **Allow memory suggestions** · **Not now** · **Read how this works**.

No preselected answer. Back, closing the screen or a timeout is not agreement.
An organiser may help someone read, but cannot choose for that person.

## Proposed “Read how this works”

- **Which words.** Eligible new text answers and written transcripts of voice answers, after your
  own agreement. Not old conversations, the rest of the family's messages, raw voice recordings
  or photos. No health or other sensitive words enter this memory-suggestion process.
- **AI and people.** OpenAI helps select possible memory quotes from eligible words. A suggestion
  is not a fact about your personality. Remembering inside Vela is different from training an AI
  model; this permission does not permit model training. Provider safety-log retention is separate
  from Vela's memory retention, as described in the full notice. The new full notice must describe this purpose alongside
  the providers, countries, provider retention and founder access that apply. This draft does not
  change those arrangements or authorise a new provider.
- **Who reads.** Suggestions and kept quotes are private to you by default. Only you can choose
  to share a kept quote. Before sharing, Vela shows the current named readers. Membership or
  organiser status alone does not allow access. New members cannot read older keeps unless you
  explicitly approve those readers. Family membership changes must remain visible in Vela.
- **What stays.** A suggestion expires with its source, normally after 30 days. Keeping needs a
  separate confirmation for each exact quote. Only those words are copied for longer, not the
  whole conversation, voice recording or photo. Keeping alone grants no family access. A kept quote
  has no fixed expiry while its grants
  and family membership remain valid. It is removed if you remove it, withdraw memory permission,
  leave the family, the family is deleted, or its source is corrected/deleted in Vela. Normal
  30-day clearing of the original words does not remove an explicitly kept quote.
- **Changing your mind.** “Turn off and clear memories” stops new suggestions and removes your
  suggestions and kept copies in Vela. It does not delete your ordinary chats or change your other
  consent choices. A later yes starts with later answers; it does not restore removed memories.
- **Payment.** Memory discovery is a Light feature. Reading already kept quotes, removing them,
  withdrawing and getting your own data copy remain free. Cancelling a subscription stops new
  paid processing after its grace period; it is not withdrawal and does not delete kept quotes.
- **Copies elsewhere.** Removing a Vela memory cannot erase copies people have saved outside Vela.
  Deleting a Telegram message does not automatically remove a kept quote in Vela; use Vela's
  memory controls too. Vela deletion does not erase messages in people's Telegram chats.
- **Records of your choice.** Vela retains a content-free consent proof under the notice's consent
  record rules, including after withdrawal. The proof is not a saved copy of your answer.

These controls and readable data-copy delivery must actually work before this wording is shown.
The existing conversation and story-book rules are otherwise unchanged.

## Separate confirmation for one kept quote

Show the exact selected quote, its source and its author. New proposed memory.keep@2 private
keeping explanation (the old family-wide memory.keep@1 explanation must not authorise it):

> Only these words will stay in your private memories after the conversation’s 30 days. Keeping does not share them with family. You can remove this memory later.

Actions: **Keep these words** · **Back**.

Sharing requires another confirmation showing the exact quote and named current readers, backed
by separate revocable audience grants. Do not label the private keep action “Keep and share”.
Report/search/export routes must enforce the same audience; they cannot reveal a private memory.

No edit to the quote inside confirmation. Changed source, expired permission or mismatched
confirmation version cancels the save. Only a server-confirmed result may say the words were kept.

## Separate withdrawal confirmation

### Turn off and clear your memories?

Vela will stop making memory suggestions from your answers. Your suggestions and kept quotes
will be removed from Vela. Your ordinary chats and other choices will not change.

This cannot erase copies people have saved outside Vela. Turning memories on again later will
not bring these memories back.

Actions: **Turn off and clear** · **Keep my current settings**.

After confirmation: **Your memory permission and copies have been cleared in Vela.**
Do not show success before the server confirms. On failure, explain that the change is unconfirmed
and offer retry with the same request key. Refresh current state after any old receipt is replayed.

## Requirements before anyone can accept

1. Founder approved the private-first product direction. Review these revised participant words
   before recording real acceptance. Direction approval is not legal clearance or activation.
2. Review and approve a new full notice and consent script, with the memory purpose, retention,
   family visibility, AI processing, provider details and withdrawal/data-copy arrangements.
   The main app permission must not silently substitute for the author's memory permission.
3. Native review of Traditional Chinese wording and an actual language-specific version/hash
   before use in that language. The English-only pilot is not silently expanded.
4. Build the app and author-verified Telegram paths, including No, Back, source, keep, remove,
   withdraw and free readable copies. No organiser/founder impersonation as acceptance.
5. Bind each acceptance to a fresh, expiring, one-use presentation: purpose, exact language/text
   fingerprint, notice version, current author/family and current privacy generation. A late tap
   on a pre-withdrawal screen must not re-enable memories. Only later answers qualify; old
   selections and old keep grants cannot return. Capture content-free channel/message evidence.
6. Prove ordinary/sensitive screening before any eligible words reach the memory model. Unknown
   sensitivity fails closed. No model may approve its own input. No health guidance is enabled.
7. Verify deletion/withdrawal, source correction, membership change and acceptance races; expired
   callbacks; failure/retry; account change; family visibility; paid lapse and readable data-copy
   delivery. Keep native accessibility, actual messenger, capacity and real-family evidence separate.
8. Update and approve the environment switch/pinning tests only in a separate activation change.
   No production tag, trial, charge, notice replacement or family message comes from this draft.
