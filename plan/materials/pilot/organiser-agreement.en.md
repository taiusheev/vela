# Vela pilot: organiser agreement

Version `organiser-agreement.v1` · 17 September 2026 · goes with `privacy-notice.v1`

This is what you, the organiser, and Timur Aiusheev (the founder, who runs the pilot) agree to while your family takes part in the Vela pilot. It is written to be read in five minutes. Please read the privacy notice as well.

**Your family:** [family code] · **The person the light is for:** [Name] · **Organiser(s):** [names] · **Pilot starts:** [date]

## 1. What the pilot is

Vela is being tested with a small number of families. Each morning someone in your family asks [Name] something; [Name] answers with a tap or a voice message; the family hears the answer and replies; [Name] hears the replies the next morning. When [Name] answers, the family knows [Name] is fine. The pilot runs on Telegram, and on LINE for families in Taiwan once it is ready. We measure whether this daily exchange works, so the founder will ask you a few short questions along the way.

Vela is early software under test. Messages can be late, and some features will change during the pilot. We will tell you when something important changes.

**Who can join, for now.** Until a lawyer advises otherwise, the pilot takes only families in which [Name] and every family member in the Vela group live in Taiwan or in a US state other than Washington, and no one is a Russian citizen, because the data protection laws of other places ask for steps Vela cannot take yet. Before your setup, tell the founder where each of them lives and whether anyone is a Russian citizen. A family elsewhere waits, and the founder tells you when that changes.

## 2. What Vela does, and what it never does

- **Vela never contacts anyone automatically.** Every message to a nearby contact is sent by a person in your family. In this pilot, if a day stays quiet, you call the nearby contact yourself; Vela only shows you the names and numbers of the contacts who have agreed.
- **Vela is not a medical or emergency service.** It cannot tell whether [Name] is in danger and cannot send help. A quiet day is information for you, not an alarm. If you think [Name] needs help now, act as you would without Vela: call [Name], call a nearby contact, call emergency services.
- If [Name] has not answered about two and a half hours after the morning message, Vela sends it once more. If there is still no answer later, you receive a short note with the facts. For the first 14 days, while Vela learns [Name]'s usual time, that note waits until about eight hours after the morning message.
- If an answer mentions something like a fall, pain, a stranger at the door or a request for money, you hear about it that day. Right after [Name] says yes to the light, Vela asks [Name] separately whether it may pass on [Name]'s words about health. If [Name] says yes, you see [Name]'s own words; if [Name] says no, you get a short note, with none of [Name]'s words, saying that something [Name] said may be worth a call. Only [Name] answers that question, and Vela works the same either way. Vela does not diagnose.
- Vela never tells [Name] that anyone worried, never points out a day [Name] did not answer, and never asks [Name] why. [Name] can always ask what the family sees, and the answer includes the lines about [Name]'s week from the most recent weekly read you received, in [Name]'s language. It never includes the read's opening lines, which say on how many days [Name] answered and on how many mornings nobody in the family asked, or its suggestion of something to ask next week.
- If Vela fails to deliver the morning message, you are told once ("We couldn't reach [Name] on [channel] today"), and no quiet-day note is sent for that day.

## 3. What you agree to do

1. **Ask [Name] first.** Talk to [Name] about Vela before the founder's call. Nothing is switched on unless [Name] says yes to the founder and taps yes in the chat. You cannot say yes on [Name]'s behalf: you may show [Name] where the buttons are, but [Name] taps them. If [Name] says no, Vela deletes what you gave about [Name] at setup and keeps only a record of the no; if [Name] never answers, the same happens 30 days after the invite link expires. You can invite [Name] again later through the founder.
2. **Respect "stop".** If [Name] says stop, everything pauses. Please don't press [Name] to start again.
3. **Set up Vela first, then the family group, with care.** First send `/start` to the Vela bot in a private chat and answer its questions; at the question about nearby contacts, give up to two names, each with how they know [Name] if you like, or tap **Skip**. Never give a phone number there: a contact's number is added only after they say yes (point 4). Its last message holds a link for [Name]: keep it, and send it to [Name] only during the founder's call. Then create a new Telegram group for Vela, with family members only and without [Name], and add the Vela bot to it yourself. Before you add each adult, now or later, send them the privacy notice yourself, individually, in their language (`https://vela.vela-light.workers.dev/privacy` or `https://vela.vela-light.workers.dev/privacy/zh-TW`). Vela's first message in the group links the notice again, with a button, "I've read it": ask each adult to tap it. The founder records that each adult received the notice. Making the bot an administrator lets reactions count; it is optional. Children under 13 do not join; a parent sends their questions for them. Keep other family conversations in your usual chat.
4. **Ask every nearby contact first.** Send each one the consent message yourself (`nearby-contact-consent.en.md`) and pass their reply to the founder, with their phone number if they say yes; the founder records it. Vela shows a contact in your quiet-day notes only after their yes is recorded, and the founder deletes a contact who says no. Tell the founder if a contact asks to be removed.
5. **Tell the founder about changes that matter,** within a day if you can: [Name] goes into hospital, moves, changes phone or messaging account, travels, or passes away; someone stops taking part without leaving the family group; or you step back as organiser. When a family member leaves the family group, Vela notices and stops including them in the turns. Vela has no button for these in the pilot; the founder makes the change the same day. For travel, give the dates: morning messages keep coming, but no second message and no quiet-day note are sent on those days. When [Name] has died, the founder stops every scheduled message and nothing automated is sent again.
6. **Keep the family's words in the family.** Don't share [Name]'s answers outside the family without [Name]'s agreement.
7. **Help us learn, if you are willing.** Short calls with the founder at around day 14 and day 30, and a few questions at weeks 4 and 12. Every question is optional.

## 4. What the founder agrees to do

1. Run the pilot carefully, and answer your messages within one day.
2. Read your family's exchanges only to run the pilot and write and send your Sunday weekly read, and log each time your family's records are opened or changed.
3. Tell you the same day if something on our side affected your family (a late or missing morning message, a message that should not have been sent), and within 72 hours if personal information may have been exposed.
4. Never sell your family's information, show advertising, or use anyone's words or voice to train AI models; never use a real answer to test Vela's AI without that person's separate yes.
5. Delete information as the privacy notice says, and act on any request to see, correct or delete it.
6. Tell you before anything in this agreement or the privacy notice changes.

## 5. The pilot is free

- Taking part in the pilot is **free**. There is nothing to pay, now or later, and the founder never asks you for payment details.
- Nothing turns into a paid plan by itself. If Vela offers a paid plan after the pilot, the founder tells you what it costs, and your family joins it only if you say yes.
- On the day-30 call, the founder asks whether you would pay US$9.99 a month for Vela Light, the paid plan Vela plans to offer later. Your answer helps us decide, and it commits you to nothing.

## 6. Stopping

- You can end your family's pilot at any time by telling the founder. [Name] can stop at any time by saying "stop".
- The founder may pause the pilot for your family if something is not working safely, and will tell you why the same day.

## 7. When the pilot ends

If your family does not continue, the founder deletes your family's information within 30 days, as the privacy notice describes. If the family book exists by then (it comes later in the pilot), the founder first offers to send you the stories the family chose to keep. Messages in your Telegram or LINE chats stay there until you delete them.

## 8. Responsibility

Vela is an early test, provided as it is. Nothing in Vela replaces your own contact with [Name], a doctor, or emergency services. Because the pilot is free, the founder accepts no financial responsibility to you for it, as far as the law allows. Nothing in this agreement limits rights you have under consumer or data protection law. *[Counsel to review this section against Taiwan's Consumer Protection Act and the law where each family lives.]*
