## New section: Memories your family keeps

**What a memory is.** An organiser can tap "Keep" on one of your answers in the Vela app. That
answer, with its question and any voice note or photo in it, is then kept in the family book
instead of being deleted after 30 days. Nothing is copied: the answer simply is not cleared.

**Telling you.** You do not need to do anything. The first time your family keeps one of your
answers, Vela tells you in your chat, with how to stop it.

**Suggestions.** Vela may suggest to the organisers an answer that seems worth keeping. To do this,
OpenAI (United States) reads ordinary answers, under the same terms as the rest of Vela's AI use
described in "Why we use it". A suggestion is not a judgement about you. Answers that mention
health, the body, money, religion, politics or a loss are never sent for suggestions, and cannot
be kept with "Keep"; when Vela is unsure, it leaves the answer out. Memories are never used to
train an AI model.

**Who sees it.** The same people who already see your answers: everyone in your family's Vela
group. Suggestions are shown only to the organisers. Nobody outside the family sees them.

**How long.** A suggestion that nobody keeps disappears with the answer, after 30 days. A kept
answer stays until an organiser removes it, you write "memories off", or the family leaves Vela.

**Stopping.** Write **"memories off"** in your chat with Vela. Everything kept from your answers
(stories in the family book included) is no longer kept: anything older than 30 days is deleted
at once, the rest when its 30 days are up. No new answers can be
kept or suggested, and Vela confirms. Writing **"memories on"** lets your family keep answers
again; it does not bring back what was removed.
