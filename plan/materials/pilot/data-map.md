# Data map

Version `data-map.v1` · 13 September 2026 · for the founder, the co-founder and counsel · sources: spec §17, architecture §5 and §12, `architecture/schema.sql` with the changes in `architecture/03-code-design.md` §3, `infra/sub-processors.md`

Every category of personal data Vela handles in the pilot: where it comes from, where it lives, why, how long, who can see it, and which sub-processors touch it. The privacy notice (`privacy-notice.en.md`) is the plain-language version of this table; when one changes, change the other in the same commit.

**Retention column.** *Defined* means a source document sets it (quoted). *Pilot rule* means the sources leave it open and this pack sets it; the retention job in `@vela/services` (`applyRetention`) must implement it before the privacy notice is used with a family beyond the founder's own. See "Gaps" at the end.

**Who can see.** Family = members of the family's Vela group · Organisers = organisers of that family · The person = the kept-light member · Contact = a nearby contact · Founder = the founder through the admin page (every family read logged in `admin_access_log`), the Neon console (logged by hand, `infra/runbooks/data-requests.md`) or the admin conversation on Telegram (row 25, not logged) · Co-founder = the AI coding agent, content-free data only (see `infra/README.md`, access rules).

**Sub-processors.** CF = Cloudflare (Workers, Durable Objects, Queues, R2, Hyperdrive) · Neon · Anthropic · Deepgram · Sentry · TG = Telegram · LINE (from sprint 2). Details and regions: `infra/sub-processors.md`.

## End of the pilot without continuing

Within 30 days the family's rows are deleted (`infra/runbooks/data-requests.md` K). Kept longer, each for its own period above: rows 16 (without names or output), 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22. The privacy notice lists the same exceptions.

## What the AI sees

Per spec §17 and architecture §12: the answer (text or transcript), the ask, the family's replies, the member list with first names, roles and languages, and memory facts. Never billing data, never nearby contacts' numbers, never the whole archive. Every call is logged in `ai_calls` with its prompt version (row 16).

## Gaps found while building this map

1. **Retention the sources leave open.** Schema retention covers media, transcripts, left members, deleted families and events only. The text of asks, replies, translations, chips, suggestions, `outbound.payload`, `ai_calls.output`, `message_refs`, `invites`, the reply text in `quiet_events.ask_to_check` and Durable Object storage have no rule. This pack sets pilot rules (rows 3, 4, 7–10, 12–14, 16, 17); `applyRetention` must implement them, with retention tests. Flows §3.15 (`architecture/04-instrument-flows.md`) and build plan 2.8 still list only media, answer transcripts and text, members, onboarding sessions, invites and events, so the job as specified would not keep the notice's 30-day promise for asks, replies, translations, chips, suggestions, `outbound.payload`, `ai_calls.output` or `message_refs`, nor delete `metrics_daily` at 24 months. Both documents need these rules before the job is built; until then the notice is not used beyond the founder's own family (`README.md`, "Before first use").
2. **"Summaries stay"** (schema) keeps a derived trace of every answer indefinitely. The notice says summaries live "while the family uses Vela"; that needs the end-of-pilot deletion job.
3. **`metrics_daily` is "kept"** (schema) while architecture §12 limits events to 24 months. The notice promises 24 months for both.
4. **Consent proof** cascades away when a member is deleted, so proof of consent disappears with the data. Acceptable for the pilot; counsel to confirm whether proof must outlive deletion.
5. **Events partitioning.** Architecture §5 and §12 drop monthly partitions after 24 months; code design §3 makes `events` a plain table. Retention becomes a delete by `at`, not a partition drop.
6. **Unrelated group messages.** The bot's group privacy mode stays on (flows §1): asks are replies to the evening prompt, `/ask` or `/later`, which privacy mode delivers. But reactions count only when the bot is a group administrator, and Telegram delivers every group message to an administrator bot whatever the privacy setting. The notice says so and promises everything not meant for Vela is discarded without being stored or logged; the inbound handler must do exactly that (flows §5, "anything else: ignore").
7. **Golden set in git** (architecture §9.3, build plan "ten real, consented cases" weekly) conflicts with provable deletion (constraint 6): only truly anonymised text may be committed, and voice never.
8. **OpenAI and Groq** receive audio in architecture §9.2 and build plan 2.5 but are missing from the architecture §12 sub-processor list.
9. **Health words** in `answers.mentions`, `flag_reason` and `memory_facts` (kind `health`): whether these are "medical" data under PDPA Article 6 needs counsel's view before memory ships.
10. **Pilot research data** (UCLA-3, call notes) and the pilot fee record live outside the schema; [NOTES TOOL] must be named in the notice before use.
11. **Nearby contacts at setup.** Flows §3.1 asks the organiser for nearby contacts during setup and inserts them without consent, and §3.12 lists every nearby contact in the quiet notice without checking `consented_at`. The pack's rule (row 6) holds only because organisers tap Skip and the founder deletes any contact typed there. The flows need either no nearby step or contacts stored as not consented, and `quiet.nearby` limited to `consented_at IS NOT NULL`.
12. **Region.** Flows §3.1 sets `families.region` from the country (`us`, `eu`), while only the `apac` database exists and the notice says the database is in Singapore. Every pilot family must be `apac` until the region router (build plan 2.2) exists; the founder checks after each setup (`infra/runbooks/data-requests.md` A). Flows §3.1 needs the same rule, or a relabelling step before the `eu` and `us` databases hold anyone.
13. **No admin write path.** The admin route is a read-only page (code design §9) and no flow writes `pilot`, `privacy_notice` or `nearby` consents, organiser-set away periods, `deceased`, or a member leaving. The founder does these in the Neon console, logging each family touched by hand (`infra/runbooks/data-requests.md`). Admin actions that write `admin_access_log` would remove the manual step.
14. **Departures.** Vela receives no update when someone leaves a group (`chat_member` is not subscribed; service messages are dropped), so nothing sets `members.left_at`. The notice asks people to tell the founder, who sets it.
15. **The admin conversation** (row 25) holds verbatim flag quotes and weekly-read drafts outside `admin_access_log` and outside `applyRetention`. The pack relies on the founder deleting them within 30 days; sending only an id or an admin-page link would remove the copy.
16. **AI failures are not retried.** `@vela/ai` resolves every provider error to its safe default, so `understandAnswer` completes with no flag and summary "answered", and nothing runs the flag check again. Flows §3.10 needs a re-run for answers whose AI calls failed; until then the founder reads those answers by hand (`infra/runbooks/incident.md`).
17. **What the family sees.** Spec §9 promises the last weekly read with the summaries; flows §3.13 returns summaries only, and `weekly_reads` does not hold the text the founder actually sent. The notice follows the flows.
