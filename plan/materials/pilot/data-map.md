# Data map

Version `data-map.v1` · 13 September 2026 · for the founder, the co-founder and counsel · sources: spec §17, architecture §5 and §12, `architecture/schema.sql` with the changes in `architecture/03-code-design.md` §3, `infra/sub-processors.md`

Every category of personal data Vela handles in the pilot: where it comes from, where it lives, why, how long, who can see it, and which sub-processors touch it. The privacy notice (`privacy-notice.en.md`) is the plain-language version of this table; when one changes, change the other in the same commit.

**Retention column.** *Defined* means a source document sets it (quoted). *Pilot rule* means the sources leave it open and this pack sets it; the retention job in `@vela/services` (`applyRetention`) must implement it before the privacy notice is used with a family beyond the founder's own. See "Gaps" at the end.

**Who can see.** Family = members of the family's Vela group · Organisers = organisers of that family · The person = the kept-light member · Contact = a nearby contact · Founder = the founder through the admin page, every family read logged in `admin_access_log` · Co-founder = the AI coding agent, content-free data only (see `infra/README.md`, access rules).

**Sub-processors.** CF = Cloudflare (Workers, Durable Objects, Queues, R2, Hyperdrive) · Neon · Anthropic · Deepgram · Sentry · TG = Telegram · LINE (from sprint 2). Details and regions: `infra/sub-processors.md`.

## What the AI sees

Per spec §17 and architecture §12: the answer (text or transcript), the ask, the family's replies, the member list with first names, roles and languages, and memory facts. Never billing data, never nearby contacts' numbers, never the whole archive. Every call is logged in `ai_calls` with its prompt version (row 16).

## Gaps found while building this map

1. **Retention the sources leave open.** Schema retention covers media, transcripts, left members, deleted families and events only. The text of asks, replies, translations, chips, suggestions, `outbound.payload`, `ai_calls.output`, `message_refs`, `invites`, the reply text in `quiet_events.ask_to_check` and Durable Object storage have no rule. This pack sets pilot rules (rows 3, 4, 7–10, 12–14, 16); `applyRetention` must implement them.
2. **"Summaries stay"** (schema) keeps a derived trace of every answer indefinitely. The notice says summaries live "while the family uses Vela"; that needs the end-of-pilot deletion job.
3. **`metrics_daily` is "kept"** (schema) while architecture §12 limits events to 24 months. The notice promises 24 months for both.
4. **Consent proof** cascades away when a member is deleted, so proof of consent disappears with the data. Acceptable for the pilot; counsel to confirm whether proof must outlive deletion.
5. **Events partitioning.** Architecture §5 and §12 drop monthly partitions after 24 months; code design §3 makes `events` a plain table. Retention becomes a delete by `at`, not a partition drop.
6. **Unrelated group messages.** For Vela to see messages starting "ask:" the bot's group privacy mode must be off, and for reactions the bot must be a group administrator, so Telegram delivers every group message. The notice promises everything else is dropped without being stored or logged; the inbound handler must do exactly that.
7. **Golden set in git** (architecture §9.3, build plan "ten real, consented cases" weekly) conflicts with provable deletion (constraint 6): only truly anonymised text may be committed, and voice never.
8. **OpenAI and Groq** receive audio in architecture §9.2 and build plan 2.5 but are missing from the architecture §12 sub-processor list.
9. **Health words** in `answers.mentions`, `flag_reason` and `memory_facts` (kind `health`): whether these are "medical" data under PDPA Article 6 needs counsel's view before memory ships.
10. **Pilot research data** (UCLA-3, call notes) and the pilot fee record live outside the schema; [NOTES TOOL] must be named in the notice before use.
