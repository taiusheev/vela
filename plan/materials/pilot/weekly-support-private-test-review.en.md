# Proposed private-test report permission

24 October 2026. DRAFT for founder review. Not a live notice, legal clearance, translation approval, or participant consent.

Purpose `weekly.support.review@1`; proposed notice `weekly-support-private-test@1`. This supplements ordinary chat permission and does not reuse memory or health-word consent.

## The parent sees

Vela can help the people named below prepare a weekly report from ordinary messages you share after you agree. The report shows your exact words, interests and optional offers of company or practical help. A family organiser reviews each offer. Health and other sensitive messages are excluded. This is not a health assessment. No messages are sent automatically and no words go to an AI for this report. Only you and the named people can read it. New family members do not get access. Report references expire within 30 days of the original message; this does not keep messages longer. You can decline or stop at any time without losing chat. Stopping hides the report and clears its references. You can read the report yourself.

**People who can read this report**

The screen lists each current organiser by name. It never says only “your family”.

Choices: **I agree for these named people** / **No, keep reports off**.

The parent can later choose **Stop reports about me**. The first private cohort requires English-speaking parents using their own linked app accounts. No organiser may agree for the parent. Messenger-only permission is not implemented.
