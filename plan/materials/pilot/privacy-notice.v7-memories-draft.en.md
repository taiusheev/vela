## New section: Memories

**Asking first.** Memories are off until you say yes in the app. Each person answers for
themselves; an organiser cannot say yes for someone else. Saying no changes nothing else. Saying
yes does not start a trial or a payment.

**Which words.** Only text answers, and written transcripts of voice answers, that you send after
saying yes. Not older conversations, other family members' messages, voice recordings or photos.
Health and other sensitive words are never used for memories; when Vela is unsure, it leaves the
words out.

**AI.** OpenAI (United States) helps pick possible memory quotes from those words, under the same
terms as the rest of Vela's AI use described in "Why we use it". A suggestion is not a judgement
about you. Memories are never used to train an AI model.

**Who sees it.** Suggestions and kept memories are private to you. Being an organiser or a family
member gives no access. You can share one kept memory with people you name; Vela shows their
names before you confirm. People who join the family later do not see older memories unless you
share with them. You can stop sharing at any time, but someone may already have read or copied
the words.

**How long.** A suggestion disappears with your answer, normally after 30 days. If you choose
"Keep these words", only those exact words stay, with no fixed end date, until you remove them,
turn memories off, leave the family, the family is deleted, or the original answer is deleted or
corrected in Vela.

**Turning it off.** "Turn off and clear" in the app stops new suggestions and deletes your
suggestions and kept memories in Vela. It does not delete your ordinary chats. Turning memories
on again later does not bring old ones back. Removing, turning off and getting a copy of your own
memories are always free.

**Records.** Vela keeps a short proof that you said yes or no (when, which wording, a fingerprint
of the text you saw), not a copy of your answers, under the consent-record rules in "How long we
keep it".
