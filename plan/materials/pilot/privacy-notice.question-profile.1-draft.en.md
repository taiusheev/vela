# Optional saved question choices — draft supplement question-profile.1

**DRAFT, 31 October 2026. Not approved, served, or active for real families.** This supplement
requires founder approval, matching Chinese review, and notice to families before activation.
It supplements, and does not replace, the current pilot notice. The saved-profile and reviewed-draft
switches remain OFF in development, staging and production; production also refuses activation.

You may choose **Save these choices for 30 days** while preparing a question for one parent.
We save the name you call them, your selected comfort level and usual contact frequency, up to two
short examples of your own messages, topics to leave out, words you do not use, and question language.
We do not save the topic of this particular draft in these choices. Please leave out health details,
other people's messages, and private details that are not needed to phrase a question.

The setup belongs to your account and this parent. Other family members cannot read it through
Vela's app, including siblings and the parent. Authorized database operation still follows the
pilot notice. Words and selected values are encrypted in the family's regional database with Neon;
Cloudflare operates the app service. We do not import a chat history or infer a personality.

Loading choices does not call AI. When you explicitly prepare a draft, the displayed choices and
your one-time question topic go to Vela's configured AI provider, under the processor terms and
locations in the pilot notice. Saving alone does not send these choices to AI. Each draft still
needs your review and approval. These choices do not change automatic questions or morning check-ins.

Choices expire 30 days after your most recent explicit save. Opening or using them does not renew
the date. Expired choices cannot be read or used; the next healthy daily retention run physically
removes the row. You can edit and explicitly save again, view a copy, or choose **Forget saved
choices**. Forgetting removes the stored words and selections immediately. For up to 30 days after
forgetting, we retain only the account/family/pair identifiers, a random revision, notice version,
and change/expiry dates so an older request cannot silently restore forgotten choices. The next
healthy retention run removes that metadata when due. Retry receipts contain operation hashes and
success metadata, not the choices; their existing 24-hour retry window and retention rules apply.

Leaving the family, removing a person, turning off their light or withdrawing light consent,
changing the linked account, account deletion, and family deletion erase affected saved choices.
A pause blocks new drafts and saves; existing choices remain private until forgotten or expired.
Questions you separately reviewed and scheduled remain scheduled after forgetting choices. You can
remove those questions in your schedule. Unsent AI candidates are not stored as a draft archive.
A data-copy request includes only your own saved choices, never another person's private setup.
