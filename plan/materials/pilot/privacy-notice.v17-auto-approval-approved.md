# Privacy notice v17: families start as soon as they are set up

Status: **approved by the founder on 9 November 2026** ("yes, change all that needed and deploy it") and merged into privacy-notice.en.md and .zh-TW.md. Goes with ADR-62 (`PILOT_FAMILY_APPROVAL=auto`). The Chinese was checked by Claude, as the founder asked on 27 October 2026; the native reader has not reviewed it yet.

What changes from v16: only point 8 of "Why we use it". Before, a family started only after the founder approved it. Now it starts as soon as it is set up, and the founder reviews every new family afterwards and removes one that cannot take part. The consent script now goes with v17; its wording is unchanged.

## English

> Each family can start as soon as it is set up. This describes the proposed cohort, not legal clearance. The founder reviews every new family on Vela's admin page, checks each participant’s residence and any relevant citizenship restrictions, and removes a family that cannot take part; everything automatic for it stops at once.

## 繁體中文

> 每個家庭設定好之後就可以開始。這是說明預定的參加者，不代表法律上的許可。創辦人會在 Vela 管理頁面查看每個新家庭，確認每位參加者的居住地，以及任何相關的國籍限制；不能參加的家庭會被移除，所有自動進行的事也會立刻停止。
