## New section, after "Questions Vela writes for an organiser"

## The photo box

Anyone in the family who uses the app can put up to 40 photos in the family's photo box, chosen one by
one from their phone's photos. Vela never looks through a phone's photo library: it only receives the
photos a person picks. Each photo can be marked as new or old and given one line of words, such as
"Lily at the beach".

**What Vela does with it.** On mornings when nobody in the family wrote a question, about twice a week
and never two days running, Vela sends the person the light is for a photo from the box with a short
question about it, signed by Vela. An old photo asks for the story behind it; two new photos may ask
which one they like more. The family's line about a photo goes into that question as they wrote it.
A question the family writes always comes first.

**Who sees it.** Everyone in the family who uses the app sees the photos in the box, who added each one,
and when Vela last showed it. The person the light is for sees a photo when Vela sends it.

**No AI.** Photos in the box are not sent to an AI provider, and neither is the line about them. The
question is a fixed sentence in the person's language with the family's line inside it.

**How long.** A photo stays in the box until the person who added it, or an organiser, takes it out.
It is then deleted within a day, including from the morning it was shown on. Photos added by someone
who leaves the family, or deletes their account, are taken out and deleted within a day. When the
family leaves Vela, the box is deleted with everything else. The words of the morning question, like
every question, are deleted after 30 days.

**Where.** Photos in the box are stored in Vela's own storage at Cloudflare, like other photos, and are
sent to Telegram or LINE only when Vela shows them on a morning.

# 隱私權聲明 v13：家庭相片盒（草稿，待創辦人核准，尚未生效）

以下為中文初稿，上線前須由母語審閱者確認。

## 「重點摘要」新增一行

- 家人可以把照片放進 App 裡的「相片盒」。在家人沒有寫問題的早上，Vela 會從相片盒挑一兩張照片，傳給被留燈的家人。照片會一直留在相片盒裡，直到有人把它拿出來。詳見「相片盒」。

## 新增段落「相片盒」

使用 App 的家人，最多可以把 40 張照片放進家庭相片盒，照片由本人從手機相簿一張一張挑選。Vela 不會瀏覽手機相簿，只會收到本人挑選的照片。每張照片可以標示為新照片或老照片，也可以寫一句話，例如「Lily 在海邊」。

**Vela 怎麼使用。** 在家人沒有寫問題的早上，大約一週兩次、且不會連續兩天，Vela 會從相片盒挑一張照片，配上一個簡短的問題，以 Vela 的名義傳給被留燈的家人。老照片會請被留燈的家人說說照片背後的故事；兩張新照片可能會問比較喜歡哪一張。家人寫的那句話會照原樣放進問題裡。家人自己寫的問題永遠優先。

**誰看得到。** 使用 App 的家人都看得到相片盒裡的照片、每張是誰放的，以及 Vela 上次是哪天傳出。被留燈的家人在 Vela 傳出照片時看到照片。

**不使用 AI。** 相片盒裡的照片和那句話都不會傳給 AI 服務。問題是用被留燈的家人的語言寫好的固定句子，再放進家人寫的那句話。

**保存多久。** 照片會留在相片盒裡，直到放照片的人或發起人把它拿出來，之後一天內刪除，包括曾經傳出的那個早上。放照片的人離開家庭或刪除帳號時，這個人放的照片也會在一天內拿出並刪除。家庭停止使用 Vela 時，相片盒會和其他資料一起刪除。早上問題的文字和所有問題一樣，30 天後刪除。

**存放在哪裡。** 相片盒裡的照片和其他照片一樣，存放在 Vela 於 Cloudflare 的儲存空間，只有在 Vela 某天早上傳出時才會送到 Telegram 或 LINE。
