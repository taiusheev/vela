# Survey: parents who live alone (EN)

For Google Forms. One page, 3–4 minutes. Form title: "How do you know your parent is okay today?"

**Intro:**
We're two founders living abroad, and our parents stayed home alone. We're building a service that sends a short note every morning: "Mom is fine." We're not selling anything yet, just trying to understand how other families handle this. Anonymous, 3 minutes.
