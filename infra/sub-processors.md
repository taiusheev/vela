# Sub-processors

17 September 2026 · architecture §12–13 · decision L10 (`plan/materials/pilot/legal-memo.md`, C9 and C10) · one page, kept in step with `plan/materials/pilot/privacy-notice.en.md` and `plan/materials/pilot/data-map.md`

Every outside company that processes personal data for Vela. The controller during the pilot is the founder as a private individual; there is no entity yet. Each provider acts for the founder, so Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act treats what it does as the founder's own (Article 4), and the founder must supervise it and record the checks (Enforcement Rules, Article 8).

## Rules

- A provider that will receive family content is added here, and to the privacy notice, **before** it receives anything, and organisers are told at least 14 days ahead. For an EU launch the notice period becomes 30 days (architecture §12).
- **Data processing terms before real family data.** Each provider's data processing terms are in place, and the date they were accepted is recorded in "Accepted on", before real family data reaches it. Where acceptance needs a step by the founder (a click, a signature, a request), it is a founder task in [`infra/README.md`](README.md), "Founder tasks: data processing terms"; nobody writes a date here that the founder has not confirmed.
- **Checks.** "Last checked" is the date someone last read the provider's current published terms at the link given. The founder, or the co-founder on the founder's behalf, checks every row at least every six months, whenever a provider announces a change to its terms or sub-processors, and before any provider starts receiving a new kind of data; the date goes in the column (Enforcement Rules, Article 8(3)). A check that finds a change the notice does not cover follows the notice's versioning rule (pilot pack README, "Versions").
- Providers receive the minimum: the AI never receives nearby contacts' numbers or billing data; logs and error reports never carry message content, transcripts, phone numbers or tokens.

## Outside this list

- **Payments:** none. The pilot is free for families, so no payment provider processes anything for Vela until billing opens with the entity (RevenueCat and the app stores, above).
- **Uptime monitoring:** no outside monitoring service. The pilot Worker keeps its own heartbeat, and the GitHub watchdog above reads it.
