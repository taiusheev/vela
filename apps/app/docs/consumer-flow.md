# Consumer flow: how families use Vela, and the app shaped around it

Written 17 October 2026 after the founder's notes on TestFlight build 5. It lists the ways a family
actually uses Vela (120 of them), how often each happens, where the app served it before, and where
it lives now. The screen changes in this branch come from this map, not the other way round.

## Who uses the app

| Person | Who they are | What they come for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organiser** | The adult child who set Vela up; told when a morning goes quiet; usually pays | "Is Mom all right today, and what did she say?" then a quick word back |
| **Sibling** | Another family member in the group | Read her words, reply, take a turn asking |
| **Turn holder** | Whoever asks tomorrow (any of the above) | One good question for her morning, in their own voice |
| Mom (kept-light member) | Lives alone; uses Telegram or LINE, not this app | A message from her child each morning, and someone answering her back |
| Nearby helper | A neighbour Mom chose | Only asked to look in on a quiet morning (messenger, not the app) |

## The 120 ways

How often: **D** daily, **W** weekly, **O** occasionally (monthly or on an event), **R** rare
(setup, trouble). *Before* is where the app served it in build 5; *Now* is where it lives after
this change.

## What the map says

1. **Two thirds of the visits are the same three steps, every day:** glance (is she all right?),
   read (what did she say?), answer (a heart or a few words). Rows 16–21, 26, 29, 35–36, 38–42, 49,
   52. They have to take two taps at most, and the answer has to reach her *now*, not in tomorrow's
   read-back. That is the chat.
2. **One step a day belongs to the turn holder:** tomorrow's question (53–56, 66–67). It stays on
   Today's tomorrow card, and the suggestion must sound like a son or daughter writing to their
   mother, not like a survey.
3. **The weekly look is its own moment** (93–99). The lit windows are a weekly picture, read on
   Sunday, filling from Monday on the left. They were at the bottom of Today as a rolling seven
   days that lit from the right.
4. **Keeping is rare but precious** (83–92). It needs its own tab with her words, photos, voice,
   stories and recipes, searchable, rather than a 30-day log of cards and a button to a book.
5. **Settings are rare** (105–113). Nothing about Mom's morning time belongs on the first screen.
   They live on You, with a shortcut in the chat menu.

## The app after this change

```
Today ──────────────── glance + today's words + tomorrow's turn
  │  "Talk with Mom" ─────► Chat with Mom (stack screen)
  │                            ├─ bubbles: Mom left (her words in Literata), you right,
  │                            │  siblings left with their names, Vela's question centred
  │                            ├─ composer: words go to her messenger now
  │                            ├─ + : question / voice / photos for her morning ─► Ask
  │                            └─ menu: her mornings, away, call
  │  tomorrow card ───────► Ask (child-voice suggestion)
Memories (was Exchanges) ── Her words (week groups, filters, search)
  │                          Family book (stories, recipes, coming up)
Sunday ─────────────── This week's windows (Mon → Sun) · weekly read · story day
You ────────────────── family, Mom's mornings, nearby, away, notifications, privacy
```

### Chat, compared with the apps people already use

| Pattern | Seen in | Kept for Vela |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your messages right, theirs left | iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE | Yes: you right in teal, Mom left on paper white |
| Consecutive messages grouped, name once | Messenger, Signal | Yes: within 5 minutes, one name, one time |
| Centred day chips | WhatsApp, Telegram | Yes: "Today", "Yesterday", "Mon 14 Oct" |
| Status under your last message only | iMessage | Yes: Sending… / Delivered / Seen 8:12 / Not delivered |
| Reactions under the bubble | iMessage, Telegram | Yes: ❤️ 😂 🤗 under Mom's message, the family's names |
| Composer pill, + on the left, send appears with text | iMessage, WhatsApp | Yes; + opens "for her morning" |
| Read receipts that guilt | WhatsApp blue ticks | No: "Seen" only when her messenger says so, never "read" |
| Typing indicators, last seen | WhatsApp, Telegram | No: Vela never shows her activity (why-vela.md) |
| Stickers, GIFs | LINE, Telegram | No: her mornings stay words, voice and photos |

Her words use Literata, as everywhere else in the app: in a chat, the parent's voice still reads as
hers. A morning question shows as what it is ("Her morning · Anna asked") so a scheduled question
and a message sent now are never confused.

### What reaches her, and when

| From the chat | Reaches her | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Words in the composer | Now, on her messenger, signed "— Mia" | `POST …/exchanges` with `when: now` |
| ❤️ 😂 🤗 on her message | In her next morning's read-back, for her latest day | `POST /v1/exchanges/:id/replies` |
| + Question / voice / photos | On the morning you choose | Ask |
| Before she says yes | On her first morning | `when: whenever` |

Her replies within 36 hours attach to the latest message she was sent (flows §3.9), so a reply to
something sent from the chat lands under it, and the conversation reads in order.

## Not in this change

- Sharing an answer outside the family (row 90) and the printed book (row 92).
- Showing a message she sends with nothing delivered in the past 36 hours: it still goes only to
  the family group (flows §3.9, unattached).
- Voice or photo sent *now* from the chat: the API sends only words now; both go to her morning.
- LINE: each chat message is a push message against the official account's monthly quota. LINE is
  off in production; this needs a cap before Taiwan.
