Working-age migrants with a living parent at home plausibly number 100M+, but nobody publishes "migrants with a parent living alone" — you would have to derive it (e.g., Indian diaspora × share with widowed parent × share of those parents living alone, which in India is far lower than in the West). Treat as a wedge, not a sized market.

## 10 hard truths for a founder entering this market

1. **The user won't buy, and the buyer won't use.** 80+ parents in Japan use a smartphone for internet at 19%; US solo elders have a 20% poverty rate. The adult child pays; the product must run on whatever is already in the parent's home (landline, flip phone, plug, camera).
2. **The honest comparable is PERS (~$4–8B global, ~5% CAGR), not "AgeTech ($2T)."** Japan's entire 見守り market is ~¥230–380億 (~$150–250M) against 7M+ solo elders — penetration of a few percent after 20 years.
3. **Amazon already tried your bundle with free distribution and lost.** Alexa Together died inside a division losing billions; Beside Care's post-mortem — emergencies and daily reassurance are different jobs — is the cheapest lesson available.
4. **Payer channels are cheap to acquire and catastrophic to lose.** Papa lost ~36 payer/employer contracts in one renewal cycle after one Bloomberg story. If you go B2B2C, keep direct revenue too.
5. **LTV is capped by the parent's trajectory.** Every customer churns via move, facility, or death; median tenure is a few years. Price and CAC must work at 24–36-month LTV.
6. **Funding is drying up for exactly this category.** AgeTech dollars fell 48% Jan–Aug 2026; the $50M+ rounds went to B2B products sold to facilities (SafelyYou, TSOLife), not to consumer peace-of-mind.
7. **Willingness to pay clusters at $10–35/month.** Japan Post ¥1,070–3,460, AloneAssist $14.99, Lively/PERS ~$25–40. Anything above requires a payer.
8. **Your quantifiable value is minutes-to-help, not "wellness."** 1 in 5 fallers lies >1h; mortality 12% if found <1h vs 67% after 72h; 21,900 Japanese found 8+ days after death. Sell reduction in time-to-discovery and prove it.
9. **Market data is unreliable enough that you must generate your own.** Orlov: the same 75+ smartphone question returns 62–78% across "valid" sources. Etkin: founders "build first and validate second." Run a paid pilot before writing the deck.
10. **The wedge that has actually scaled is municipal/insurer-funded, low-tech, and boring** (SKT + local governments, Japan Post visits, PERS on Medicaid waivers, MA flex cards). Design so a city, a D-SNP, or a postal service can buy it in bulk, and let the adult-child DTC channel be the proof, not the plan.

**Verification flags:** Alexa Together price/exact end date, Best Buy Health impairment/Current Health sale, Germany/Korea 70+ smartphone rates, UN 2024 migrant-origin counts, and Medicaid waiver PERS coverage were not confirmed against primary sources this session.
