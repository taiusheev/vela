## 2. Five things to borrow

1. **Buyer ≠ user, and the recipient needs no app.** Storyworth (email reply), Kinsome (email/SMS), Enrich (LINE bot) all put zero install burden on the elder. Famileo went further: paper. For Vela: parent stays inside Telegram/MAX/WhatsApp; only the child installs anything.
2. **One fixed daily moment.** BeReal: 68% open within 3 min of push, 72% daily engagement among the core. Storyworth: exactly one question per week for a year. Vela's "every morning at 9" ritual is the proven shape; keep it to one prompt, not a feed.
3. **Contributor virality = the growth engine.** Aura: 50% of 2024 sales came from private family-invite networks; Famileo shows ~8 active users per paying family. Design the sibling/grandchild invite loop from day one (the child pays, siblings contribute, cousins are in the escalation ladder anyway).
4. **Price the *escalation*, not the messaging.** Snug's free tap is free; the $19.99/mo tier is "a human calls, then a wellness check." Chikaku/Docomo bundles presence + calls at ¥1,980. Vela's $10-20 should be justified by the ladder and the drift report, with messaging as the free hook.
5. **Institutional / channel distribution over consumer ads.** Famileo bootstrapped through 2,700 care homes; Chikaku sells through every Docomo shop with Aflac as investor; Korean care-calls scale through municipalities; Western Union piloted AXA insurance inside transfers ([AXA](https://www.axa.com/en/news/AXA-and-Western-Union-Partnership)). For Vela: remittance apps, diaspora employers, and relocant communities are the analogue.

## 3. Five things to avoid

1. **Hardware.** GrandPad ($299 + $40-59/mo) gets "too expensive"; Skylight's store revenue expected to drop 20-50% in 2025; Konnekt A$990; Zojirushi 14k contracts in 23 years. Vela is software on a phone the parent already owns.
2. **Bare check-in pings.** Enrich's free "OK?" bot attracted a user base that is only 26% 65+. A ping with nothing inside is ignored by elders; the sign-of-life must ride on content they *want*.
3. **Mechanical care voice.** Korean elders push back on AI care calls as "기계적 돌봄." Vela's daily message must be authored (or at least voiced) by the family; AI only drafts and nudges the child.
4. **Freemium messaging with hope of conversion.** Marco Polo: 56M installs, 9 product iterations, profitability only in 2024, ~$10M/yr run-rate; Locket: 80M downloads → 100k payers (~0.1%). Don't be a messenger; be a paid service with a messenger as the pipe.
5. **Single-channel dependence in Russia.** WhatsApp fully blocked 11 Feb 2026 ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/12/tech/russia-whatsapp-social-media-clampdown-intl)); Telegram officially throttled from 10 Feb 2026, calls blocked since Aug 2025, full block floated for Sept 2026 ([Bitrix24 journal](https://www.bitrix24.ru/journal/blokirovka-telegram/)); MAX removed from app stores in June 2026 and sanctioned in July ([Habr](https://habr.com/ru/news/1061678/)). Build channel-agnostic (Telegram + MAX + SMS/voice fallback).

## 5. Channel insights

- **Russia is now MAX-first for reach, Telegram-first for daily habit.** MAX overtook Telegram on monthly reach in May/June 2026; Telegram still leads daily reach (72M vs 61M in March). WhatsApp collapsed to 30M daily. A Vela bot must exist on both MAX and Telegram; MAX has an official bot/Mini App API at [dev.max.ru](https://dev.max.ru/) with CRM integrations and agencies already migrating bots from Telegram ([Sostav](https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/282100/83880)). Risk: MAX is state-run (age ID via Gosuslugi, [Techora](https://techora.ru/news/max-vvel-tsifrovoy-id-dlya-podtverzhdeniya-2026-07-24)) — assume every message is readable.
- **Telegram Bot API costs nothing per message** (rate limits ~30 msg/s, [Bots FAQ](https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq)); versus WhatsApp Business API since Jul 2025 charging per delivered template: utility $0.0014 India / $0.0034 US / $0.0077 rest-of-world; marketing $0.0118 India / $0.025 US / $0.0604 RoW; free 24h window closes for utility on 1 Oct 2026 ([ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/), [Blueticks](https://blueticks.co/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-2026)). At 1 utility msg/day per parent, WhatsApp costs ≈$0.04-0.23/mo per family — negligible vs $10-20 price, but WhatsApp is unusable in Russia and the parent must have messaged the business first for free-form replies. For Philippines/India, WhatsApp is fine; note marketing-category classification risk if the daily message is deemed promotional.
- **The daily message should be a *reply-to-child* thread, not a bot broadcast.** Enrich and Snug show tap-to-confirm works; Korea shows elders resent AI-voiced care; Famileo/Storyworth show elders engage when the content is from family. Put the bot in a 3-party group (child + parent + Vela) so the tap/reply is social, and the bot only watches silence.
- **Remittance apps are the acquisition rail.** Paysend (10M), Remitly (9.3M active), Wise (15.5M), KoronaPay (26M txns/yr) already own the "adult child abroad paying for parent at home" relationship; WU×AXA proves insurers will bundle into transfers. Pitch Vela as the "care add-on" line item on the transfer receipt; Chikaku's Aflac investment shows insurers see this category.
- **B2B2C in CIS = not care homes.** Famileo's 2,700 EHPADs have no CIS equivalent at scale; the analogous institutions are relocant employers (IT firms in Yerevan/Tbilisi/Almaty), diaspora Telegram communities, and Uzbek/Tajik/Kyrgyz migrant networks in Russia (reverse direction: worker in Moscow, parent in a kishlak) where KoronaPay dominates.

**Gaps not closed in the time box:** GrandPad and Famileo retention/churn (not public); Chikaku household counts (only "3x in a year" and a 14M-senior-household TAM); Marco Polo Plus conversion rate; KakaoTalk-native care features; primary source for the Mediascope 65+ split.
